The details that matter.

        

          
What belongs in a grain enquiry?
The product, estimated quantity, town or delivery area and preferred timing. If your organisation needs recurring supply, include the likely frequency.

          
What belongs in a vending enquiry?
The building type, location, approximate daily footfall and any potential installation area you already have in mind.

          
Does the form confirm an order?
No. It only prepares a WhatsApp enquiry. An order or vending arrangement is confirmed after the details and commercial terms are agreed directly.
