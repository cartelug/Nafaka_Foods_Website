A fit for the right setting

Before a machine moves in.

Which locations can be considered?

The proposal covers commercial and institutional settings such as office buildings, hospitals, universities, churches, banks, airports, industrial facilities and government offices. Every location is assessed before agreement.

What does the building provide?

A suitable agreed space, access to power and support for the equipment’s safety and security. Rent, utilities, access and the wider commercial arrangement are agreed with Nafaka.

Can the service operate after normal hours?

The machines can support extended-hours access where the building’s access, power, security and agreed operating conditions allow it.

Who restocks and maintains the machine?

Under the proposed managed model, Nafaka handles stock replenishment, monitoring, preventive maintenance and technical and customer-care support.
