A clear working arrangement

We manage the service.
You provide the setting.

Nafaka’s role

  • Supply, install and commission the machine
  • Stock products and manage replenishment
  • Monitor inventory and machine operations
  • Carry out preventive maintenance
  • Provide technical and customer-care support

The building’s role

  • Provide an agreed, suitable installation space
  • Provide access to a suitable power connection
  • Support the safety and security of the equipment
  • Agree access and operating arrangements
  • Agree rent and utility terms with Nafaka

Commercial terms are negotiated. The proposal allows for landlord rent or an agreed revenue-sharing model, with electricity charges agreed separately. No specific return, free installation or zero-cost arrangement is promised here.

Before we begin

Your building.
Your questions.

Is vending already Nafaka’s main business?

No. Nafaka’s core business is grain trading, with rice at its heart. Managed snacks and drinks vending is a newly introduced service.

Does our team need to restock the machine?

Under the proposed managed model, Nafaka handles stock and replenishment, together with monitoring, maintenance and support.

What does the building need to provide?

A suitable agreed space, power connection and equipment security. Nafaka and the building representative agree rent, utilities, access and other commercial terms.

Can we choose the products or payment options?

These are discussed during assessment and agreement. The proposal covers sealed snacks and cold drinks, with cash and cashless options subject to the selected equipment.
