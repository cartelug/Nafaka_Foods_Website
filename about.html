Our story

A decade in grain.
A new chapter beginning.

Founded in 2016, Nafaka Foods began as a grain supply and distribution business rooted in trust — sourcing, grading and delivering quality rice and staple grains across Uganda. That same reliability now extends into a new venture: fully managed smart vending for commercial buildings, a young second arm of the business we're proud to be building.
